Who We Are

ACORD Uganda is committed to supporting community-led development and addressing social and economic challenges affecting communities.

We believe in working collaboratively with communities, institutions and development partners to achieve sustainable change.

Our Vision, Mission and Values

Our Vision

A society where all people enjoy dignity, equality, justice and opportunities.

Our Mission

To work with communities and partners to promote inclusive and sustainable development.

Our Values

Participation, accountability, equality, transparency and respect for human dignity.
